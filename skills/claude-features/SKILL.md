# 10 Claude features worth switching on this week

Made by Selr AI. Steps come from Anthropic's help centre (support.claude.com), checked 8 October 2026. Claude changes often: if a menu looks different, follow what's on screen and check the help centre.

## How to run it

1. Ask once, in one message: which plan they're on (Free, Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise), whether they use Claude on the web, the desktop app or the phone app, whether they use Gmail and Google Calendar, and what their business does.
2. Show the ten features below by name only, marking the ones their plan doesn't include. Ask which one to start with, or suggest starting at 1.
3. For each feature: say in one sentence what it does for their business, give the steps to switch it on, then give one first thing to try using their business. Wait for them to say done before moving on.
4. Keep every step short and in plain English. Never change a setting for them, never connect an account for them, and never send, book or delete anything.
5. On Team or Enterprise, an owner or admin may need to switch some of these on first. Say so when it applies.

## The ten features

1. **Projects** (every plan; Free can make up to five). One home per client or job, with its own files and instructions, so you stop re-explaining. Open **Projects**, then **New project**. Add your files and set the project instructions. Try: a project for your biggest client with their past quotes inside.

2. **Connectors** (Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive; Team and Enterprise need an admin to turn them on). Claude can search your email, calendar and files. Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**, and connect your Google account. In a chat, the **+** menu, then **Connectors**, switches each one on or off. By default Claude asks before it sends, creates or changes anything. Try: "What did I promise customers in email this week?"

3. **Research** (paid plans; needs web search on). Claude runs several searches that build on each other and comes back in minutes with sources you can check. In a chat, click **+**, then **Research**. Try: "Compare the three main suppliers for [product] in [city], with sources."

4. **File creation** (every plan). Claude makes real Excel, Word, PowerPoint and PDF files you can download. Open **Settings**, then **Capabilities**, and switch on **Code execution and file creation**. Try: "Turn this price list into an Excel sheet with a total column."

5. **Skills** (every plan; needs code execution on). Skills give Claude a set way of doing a job. Claude's built-in skills for Excel, Word, PowerPoint and PDF work on their own once file creation is on. Open **Customize**, then **Skills**, to switch skills on or off or upload your own. Try: write down how you do a quote, step by step, and ask Claude to help turn it into a skill.

6. **Artifacts** (every plan; needs code execution on). Claude builds a document, a one-page dashboard or a small interactive tool beside the chat, and you can share it. Ask for one in any chat, and find everything you've made in the **Artifacts** tab. Artifacts start private to you. Try: "Build a simple checklist page for opening the shop each morning."

7. **Voice mode** (every plan, in beta; works best on your phone). Talk to Claude and it answers out loud. In the phone app, tap the sound wave icon next to the microphone. On the web or desktop, it's the sound wave icon at the bottom right of the chat. Try: talk through tomorrow's jobs on the drive home.

8. **Claude in Chrome** (paid plans; Google Chrome only). A browser extension that lets Claude read pages, click, type and fill in forms. It pauses to ask you when something needs your approval. Get it from the Chrome Web Store, click **Add to Chrome**, sign in and pin it. Read Anthropic's safety guide first. Try: "Find the three cheapest delivery options on this supplier's site."

9. **Cowork** (paid plans). Hand Claude a whole task and come back to finished files, documents or research. In the message box, choose **Cowork** (on the newer layout any chat can take a Cowork task), describe the job and check its plan before it runs. On desktop it only reads and writes folders you connect. Try: "Sort this folder of receipts by month and make a summary sheet."

10. **Scheduled tasks** (paid plans). Describe a job once and Claude runs it on a schedule, such as a Monday morning briefing of your emails and calendar. In Cowork, click **Scheduled**, then **New task**, or just describe the task and how often in a chat on the newer layout. Try: "Every Monday at 7am, summarise my unread emails and this week's calendar."
