# 12 jobs to hand Claude before Christmas

Made by Selr AI.

## How to run it

1. First get the closed dates once, in one message, using this outline:
   My business: [what we do, where]. Closed from [date] to [date]. Back on [date]. Last day to order: [date]. Supplier cut-off: [date]. Holiday trading hours: [days and hours].
   Use these dates in every job. Never guess a date, price or name.
2. Show the 12 jobs below by name and ask which ones to do. Do them one at a time.
3. For each job, ask only for what is in the [brackets] that the dates didn't already answer.
4. If something is missing, leave a [gap] and list it under **Missing** at the end.
5. Finish each one with a one-line reminder: check every date before it goes out. The person posts or sends it, never you.

## The 12 jobs

1. **Christmas trading hours.** Write a short post with our Christmas trading hours: [each day and its hours]. Friendly, under 60 words.

2. **Holiday roster.** Here's who is available over the break: [names and the dates they can work]. Make a holiday roster as a table, day by day, and flag any day with nobody on.

3. **Out-of-office reply.** Write an out-of-office email reply for while we're closed. Say the date we're back and what to do if it's urgent: [what they should do].

4. **Supplier cut-off reminder.** Write a short reminder for my team: the last day to order from [supplier] before Christmas is [date]. Add what to check before ordering: [stock, jobs booked, anything else].

5. **Client thank-you email.** Write a short end-of-year thank-you email to our clients. Mention one real thing from this year: [detail]. No sales pitch, under 120 words.

6. **Staff party invite.** Write a staff Christmas party invite: [date, time, place, what to wear, RSVP by]. Warm and short.

7. **Gift card post.** Write a post about our gift cards: [what they're for, the amounts, how to buy one]. Under 60 words, one clear way to buy.

8. **Year-in-review post.** Here are the highlights from our year: [three to five dot points]. Write a year-in-review post in plain words. Use only these points, don't add any.

9. **January restart checklist.** Make a checklist for reopening on [date]. Here's what I know needs doing: [list]. Group it into first day and first week.

10. **Closed-dates sign.** Write a closed-dates sign for the shop window and the website: closed [dates], back [date]. Big, short, nothing else.

11. **Last-order date post.** Write a post telling customers the last day to order before Christmas is [date]. One clear deadline, under 50 words.

12. **Supplier thank-you note.** Write a short thank-you note to [supplier] for this year. Mention [one thing they did well]. Under 80 words.
