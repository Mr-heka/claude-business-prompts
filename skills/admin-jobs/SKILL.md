# 12 admin jobs you can hand to Claude

Made by Selr AI.

## How to run it

1. If you don't know the business yet, ask once, in one message: what the business does, who the customers are, and how they like to sound. Use the answers for every job after that.
2. Show the 12 jobs below by name only and ask which one to do first, unless the person already said.
3. For the chosen job, ask for whatever is in the [brackets] in one message, then do the job.
4. Never guess a price, rate, name or date. If something is missing, do the rest and list what's missing under **Missing** at the end.
5. Finish with a one-line reminder to check prices, names and dates before anything goes out. The person sends or posts it, never you.

## The 12 jobs

1. **Reply to a review.** Here is a Google review for my [business type]: "[paste review]". What really happened, in two lines: [your side]. Write a short, calm reply. Thank them, own one thing, and invite them to get in touch.

2. **Draft a quote.** Turn these job notes into a quote with line items, labour, materials, GST and a total. Use only my rates and prices below and list anything missing instead of guessing. Notes: [paste notes]. My rates and prices: [paste rates]. Then make it a one-page PDF I can send, and write the short email that goes with it.

3. **Summarise a meeting.** List the decisions, then the actions with who owns each one and when it's due: [paste notes or transcript]. Then write a short, friendly follow-up email to [who it's for].

4. **Write a job ad.** Write a short job ad for a [role] in [suburb or city], [hours]. Mention [what makes your team good to work for] and how to apply.

5. **Chase an invoice.** Write a polite reminder for invoice [number] for [amount], now [days] days overdue. Friendly but firm, one short paragraph, with how to pay.

6. **Turn notes into an email.** Turn these messy notes into a clear, friendly email to my client: [paste notes].

7. **Build a roster.** Build next week's roster as a table, Monday to Sunday, for these shifts: [shift times]. Staff and when they can work: [names and availability]. Rules: [for example two on every shift, nobody over five days, a senior on every opening shift]. Follow every rule and tell me if one can't be met. Then make it a spreadsheet I can download.

8. **Write your FAQs.** Write a short FAQ for our website. Use only my answers. List any question I didn't answer under Missing. Questions customers ask: [paste them]. My answers: [paste them].

9. **Compare two quotes.** Compare these supplier quotes in a short table (price, delivery, payment terms, anything else that matters) and tell me which is better value and why: [paste both].

10. **Turn steps into a checklist.** Turn these steps into a clear checklist my staff can follow: [paste the steps].

11. **Plan a week of posts.** Plan 5 Instagram post ideas for my [business type] next week, one line each, mixing behind the scenes, a tip and an offer.

12. **Answer a complaint.** Draft a calm reply to a customer who says [complaint]. Apologise, explain what we'll do next ([your fix]), and keep it short.
