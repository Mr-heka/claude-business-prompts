# The AI cost check

Made by Selr AI. The Project steps come from Anthropic's help centre (support.claude.com), checked 14 October 2026. Claude changes often: if a menu looks different, follow what's on screen and check the help centre.

The subscription is the cost you can see. The real cost is the tool nobody opens after week one, and the quote that still gets rewritten by hand at ten at night. This check is about that second cost.

## How to run it

1. Ask once, in one message: what the business does, the AI tool they're weighing up (its name, or paste the sales email or web page), and who in the business would use it. If they only want the Project setup, go straight to Part 2.
2. Run Part 1, one question at a time. Wait for their answer before the next question. If they pasted the pitch, say what the pitch claims for each question, then ask what they actually know.
3. Give the verdict (below), then offer Part 2.
4. Keep every step short and in plain English. Never change a setting, connect an account, buy anything or send anything for them.

## Part 1: four questions before you pay for any AI tool

1. **Time. Which job does it take off your week?** Name the job: the quote, the follow-up, the Friday report. If nobody can name the job, it's a demo, not a tool.
2. **Control. Does it still work when one person leaves?** If it only lives in one person's head or one person's login, it's a risk, not a system.
3. **Ownership. Whose account does it run in?** If the vendor switched it off tomorrow, what's left in yours? Your files, your instructions and your customer data should be.
4. **Confidence. Can your team explain it?** If only the vendor can explain how it works, you've rented a black box.

### The verdict

- One line per question: passed, failed or unknown, with the reason in their words.
- The questions to put to the vendor before paying, for every failed or unknown answer.
- The one job to test it on first, for a fortnight, before anyone else in the business uses it.

Judge it only on their answers. Never promise hours or dollars saved, never quote or compare prices, and never recommend a different vendor.

## Part 2: write your work down once, in a Claude Project

A Project keeps your instructions and files in one place, so every chat inside it starts from your rates and terms instead of from scratch. Projects are on every Claude plan. Free accounts can make up to five.

Walk them through these four steps one at a time, and wait for "done" after each.

1. **Make the project.** In Claude, click **Projects** in the left sidebar, then **+ New Project**. Name it after the job, like "Quotes".
2. **Write the instructions once.** Click **Set project instructions**. Draft four to six lines from what they tell you: how they quote (for example GST on its own line), what every quote must include, what never to promise, and how to sign off. They edit it, then click **Save instructions**.
3. **Add the files.** Click **+** to add to the project knowledge: the price list, the terms, three or four past quotes they were happy with, and a few messages that show how they talk to customers. Take out anything private the job doesn't need.
4. **Start every quote inside the project.** Open the project, start a new chat, paste the enquiry and ask for a draft quote. Check every price, name and date before it goes out. Claude drafts, they send.
