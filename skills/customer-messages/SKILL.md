# 10 customer messages Claude can draft for you

Made by Selr AI.

## How to run it

1. If you don't know the business yet, ask once, in one message: what the business does, the owner's name and sign-off, and two or three texts or emails they've already sent customers. Learn how they sound from those (length, greeting, sign-off, how casual) and write every message that way.
2. Show the 10 messages below by name only and ask which one to do first, unless the person already said.
3. For the chosen message, ask for whatever is in the [brackets] in one message, then write it. Keep texts under 60 words. Emails get a subject line.
4. Never guess a price, date, time, name or link. If something is missing, leave a [placeholder] and list it under **Missing** at the end.
5. Never send anything. Finish with a one-line reminder to check names, dates, prices and links. The person sends it, never you.

## The 10 messages

1. **Follow up a quote.** I sent [customer] a quote for [job] on [day] and haven't heard back. Write a short, friendly follow-up text. No pressure.

2. **Confirm a booking.** Write a booking confirmation text for [customer]: [job], [day and date], [time], at [address or suburb]. Ask them to [anything they need to do before we arrive].

3. **Appointment reminder.** Write a reminder text for [customer] about [job] on [day] at [time]. Ask them to reply YES to confirm, or let me know if they need to move it.

4. **Say you're running late.** I'm running about [minutes] late to [customer]'s job. Write a quick apology text with my new arrival time, [time]. Short, no excuses.

5. **Thanks after a job.** We finished [job] for [customer] today. Write a short thank-you text. Mention [one useful detail, like where the paperwork or the labels are].

6. **Nudge a late payment.** Invoice [number] to [customer] is [days] days overdue. Write a polite payment reminder text. Friendly, no guilt trip, and put [payment link] where the link goes.

7. **Ask for a review.** Write a short text asking [customer] for a Google review after [job]. Make it easy for them and put [review link] where the link goes.

8. **Price rise notice.** From [date] my [price or fee] is going up because [reason]. Write a short email to my regular customers. Put [new price] where the price goes.

9. **Win back a customer.** [customer] used to book us [how often] but we haven't heard from them in [how long]. Write a short, warm check-in text. No discount, no hard sell.

10. **Turn down a job.** [customer] asked us to [job we don't do]. Write a polite reply saying no, and suggest they find [the right kind of business].
