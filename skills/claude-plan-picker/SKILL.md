# Which Claude plan do you need

Made by Selr AI. Plan lines come from Anthropic's pricing page (claude.com/pricing) and help centre (support.claude.com), checked 14 October 2026. Plans change often. If anything below differs from claude.com/pricing, the pricing page wins: say so.

## The five plans, in plain English

- **Free, for trying it.** Chat on the web, desktop and phone. Search the web, create files and run code. Connect your apps and tools. Up to five Projects.
- **Pro, for everyday work.** Everything in Free, plus more usage, more Claude models, Projects, handing off and scheduling tasks, and Claude Code.
- **Max, for people who work with Claude all day.** Everything in Pro, with five or twenty times the usage of Pro, higher output limits and priority access at busy times.
- **Team, for your staff (2 to 150 people).** Central billing and administration, single sign-on, admin controls for connectors, and no model training on your content by default.
- **Enterprise, for large businesses.** Everything in Team, plus audit logs, user and organisation spend limits, role-based access and network-level access control.

## How to run it

1. Ask once, in one message: how many people in the business will use Claude, which jobs they want it for this week (name them), which plan they're on now and whether they keep hitting the usage limit, and whether they need staff accounts managed in one place or audit and access controls.
2. Pick the smallest plan that covers this week's work:
   - Trying it, or a few chats a week: **Free**.
   - Using it most days, or wanting Claude Code or scheduled tasks: **Pro**.
   - On Pro and hitting the limit most days: **Max**.
   - Two or more staff who each need their own account, with one bill and admin controls: **Team**.
   - Audit logs, spend limits or tighter access control across a large business: **Enterprise**.
3. Answer in three short parts: the pick in one line, why in two lines, and the one sign that it's time to move up.
4. The rule: start on the smallest plan that covers this week's work, and move up when the limits get in your way. Not before.

## Never

- Never quote a price, a discount or an API credit amount. For prices, point to claude.com/pricing.
- Never upgrade, downgrade or change billing for them. They make the change themselves.
- Never promise what a plan will save them.
