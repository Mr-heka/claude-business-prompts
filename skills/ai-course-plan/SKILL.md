# AI course plan

Made by Selr AI. Anthropic, the company behind Claude, runs a free Academy. This skill helps you take its small business course in a way that changes how you work, not just which buttons you press.

## The courses (free, on Anthropic's own Academy)

- **AI Fluency for Small Businesses:** https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-for-small-businesses
- **AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations** (the small business course page recommends this one first): https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-framework-foundations
- **Claude 101** (if you've never used Claude): https://anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-101

Each of these lists a certificate of completion on its course page. Check the course pages for the current details; they can change.

## How to run it

1. Ask in one message: have they used Claude before, what their business does, and one real job from the business they'd like AI to help with (a quote, a report, a weekly email, rostering).
2. Pick their starting point with the link: never used Claude, start with Claude 101. Otherwise, the small business course, noting that its page recommends Framework & Foundations first.
3. Keep their real job open the whole way. Each time they paste what a part of the course taught them, turn it into three short steps they can try on that job today.
4. When they finish, write a one-page teach-back for one person in their team: what they learnt, how the team will use it on that job, and one small exercise for that person. Teaching one person is when it sticks.
5. You haven't seen the course content. Work only from what they paste and from the course pages above. Never make up what the course says.

## The prompt

Help me do Anthropic's free course, AI Fluency for Small Businesses, the way that makes it stick.

The course: https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-for-small-businesses
If I've never used Claude: https://anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-101
The course page recommends this one first: https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-framework-foundations

1. Ask me in one message: have I used Claude before, what my business does, and one real job from my business I'd like AI to help with.
2. Tell me which course to start with, based on my answers, with the link.
3. While I go through it, I'll paste what each part taught me. Each time, show me how to use it on my real job, in three short steps I can try today.
4. When I finish, write a one-page teach-back for one person in my team: what I learnt, how we'll use it on that job, and one small exercise for them.
5. You haven't seen the course yourself, so work only from what I paste. Don't make up what it says.
