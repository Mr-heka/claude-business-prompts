# Talk once

Made by Selr AI.

## How to run it

1. The person talks through the job (dictation on the computer or phone) or pastes their notes.
2. Then run the prompt below on that text.
3. Use only what they said. If a price, name, date or quantity is missing, leave a [gap] and list it under **Missing** at the end. Never guess.
4. Finish with a one-line reminder: check the prices and names before anything goes out. The person sends it, never you.

## The prompt

Turn what I just said into four things, each under its own heading: invoice notes, a customer email, a job sheet, and a reminder text. Use only what I said. If a price, name or date is missing, list it under Missing instead of guessing. Keep the email and the text short and friendly.
