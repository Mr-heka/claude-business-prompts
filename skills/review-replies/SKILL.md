# Review replies

Made by Selr AI.

## How to run it

1. Ask for the review (paste it in) and, for a bad review, what really happened in two lines. Ask both in one message.
2. Use the matching prompt below. Never invent facts, refunds, discounts or promises the person didn't give you.
3. Keep it short. No arguing, no long explanations, no excuses.
4. Finish with a one-line reminder: check names and anything promised, then post it yourself. You never post it.

## The prompts

1. **Bad review.** Here is a review for my [business type]: "[paste the review]". What really happened, in two lines: [your side, facts only]. Write a short, calm reply. Thank them, own one thing, and invite them to get in touch.

2. **Too stiff? Follow up with:** Too stiff. Plain Aussie English, under 60 words.

3. **Good review.** Here is a five-star review for my [business type]: "[paste the review]". Write a short thank-you reply that mentions one detail from their review, under 40 words.
